Our story

He saw the truck.
Then he earned it.

One day, Garett saw a food truck and said it would be his. He turned that promise into long days, relentless work, and a kitchen on wheels built from the ground up.

Lunch Box is Garett’s way of sharing the Jamaican heritage that shaped him: delicious, uncomplicated meals cooked with the warmth and care of home. Every box is meant to feel familiar, generous, and full of the flavors he grew up with.

Today, Garett and the Lunch Box family are bringing that story to Hartford one freshly packed meal at a time.
